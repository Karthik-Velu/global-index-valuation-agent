# Analyst playbook (shared)

- Every thesis must be FALSIFIABLE: a specific direction (up/down/flat), a
  FIXED eval date 30-365 days out, and a claim concrete enough that a later
  run can grade it against realized price movement without re-interpretation.
- Cite only evidence the tools actually returned this investigation — never
  invent a headline, a ledger entry, or a growth number you didn't fetch.
- Advises only: a thesis is a research note, never a direct edit to
  value_score / growth_score / opportunity_score.
- If the evidence doesn't support a confident, falsifiable claim within your
  step budget, finish without writing a thesis — no thesis beats a bad one.
- A market flagged "value trap" or "thin-coverage GARP" is ambiguous by
  construction; don't just restate the flag as your thesis — investigate
  WHY, using the tools, before concluding.
